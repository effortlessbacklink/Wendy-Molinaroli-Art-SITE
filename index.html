Wendy Molinaroli Art

About

Wendy Molinaroli Art is a South Carolina studio and shop created by artist Wendy Molinaroli. The collection includes original artwork and art prints in watercolor, acrylic, and oil, along with unique pieces designed to bring creativity and character to homes and studios.

The shop also features vintage furniture with beautiful hand-painted clay finishes, as well as paints, pens, workbooks, and other creative supplies. Visit Wendy Molinaroli Art to explore the collection and find something special for your space or your next creative project.

FAQ

What types of artwork do you offer?

We offer original watercolor, acrylic, and oil artwork, along with art prints for homes, studios, and creative spaces.

Do you sell hand-painted furniture?

Yes, we offer vintage furniture with unique, hand-applied clay paint finishes.

What creative supplies can I find?

You can find paints, pens, workbooks, and other tools to support your own creative projects.
